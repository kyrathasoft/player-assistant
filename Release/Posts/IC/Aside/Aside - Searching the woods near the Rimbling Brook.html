The battle was won. Kelpie hacked once at the paralyzed orc spawn to make certain it would raid no more homes or caravans, then stepped back with deliberate care. Even wounded and shaken, he treated the creatures with wary respect, orcspawn were dangerous foes, and he had no intention of chasing them into whatever dark holes they called home.

He adjusted his shield, favouring his bruised side, and began the careful retreat back across the bridge.

Kelpie spared only a glance for the fallen orcspawn and their weapons. Weapons could wait; his companions’ safety could not. He nodded to Geoffroy, giving him leave to claim a sword from the dead, then pressed the recovered scroll into Jelb’s hands.
