Ch 3 - Joining the Caravan to Raven's Pass

At two hours past sunrise, dust filters out of the forest impinging on the eastern edge of the hamlet, and the farmers beneath their awnings in the square look alert, sensing the imminent arrival of the caravan from Eastdale - which means customers! A boy employed by Morgan's Morsels has taken food orders from two of the half dozen individuals standing around. Presumably they are potential guards interested in hiring on for the next leg of the journey -- Kirkilston to Raven's Pass.

The wagons emerge from the woods to the east, six of them in a queue, each pulled by a pair of mules. Driving the lead wagon is Billworth Turgen of Helix. Men walk alongside the second, fourth, and sixth wagons -- guards no doubt.

As the caravan enters the hamlet square, the wagons circle up, not for protection but out of habit. Immediately, Billworth signals for the boy from Morgan's Morsels and begins giving him instructions. The vegetable farmers smile in greeting as soon of those accompanying the caravans approach their kiosks. An attractive brunette emerges from a nearby house and runs to one of the caravan men, who sweeps her up into his arms, swings her around, then kisses her.

Ch 3 - Joining the Caravan to Raven's Pass

Jelb spent his time waiting in conversation and meditation - pursuing the first when possible, but the second in any free moments.   Arcane study was a rigorous demand of one wanted to learn and grow stronger.  For the sake of Jelenneth, and to a lesser extent to save his own face from his words the prior night, he deeply desired greater mastery.

However much he favored his studies, among men there was a still more powerful tool: conversation.  Three men against unnumbered bandits were not odds he favored.  Let alone should any of those in the party be denied as guards for any reason.

So he struck up conversation with other potential guards: introducing himself as Jelb, laying out the quest upon which he and his companions had staked their honor, and along whether any were willing to join them for reasonable compensating... or at least to not compete against them for caravan spots.

After the long march back to Kirkliston, it was a relief to rest a moment while they waited for the caravan. Kelpie made straight for the well, filling his waterskin before splashing cool water over his face and bald head. Those chosen as guards (assuming they agree to 5 gold daily, and this is -- barring mishap or delay -- a two-day gig) are:

  7. Thurgan
  8. Berric
  9. Geoffroy


So, hex-crawling is dangerous. Really, really dangerous. So dangerous, in fact, that only the very foolish (adventurers), greedy (merchants), or the foolhardy/desperate/overconfident (bandits, orcs and other humanoids, or once again adventurers) are willing to risk it. Traveling well-used roads is safer, generally, because local militias tend to sweep those portions of roads close to settlements and -- less frequently, but far more effectively -- patrols of Red Bannermen and clerics of St. Ygg make sweeps at irregular intervals. This hasn't stopped brigand and humanoid attacks, but it has curbed them in the past decade.

Departing from roads (i.e., taking a game trail through the woods, heading overland) is very dangerous. The Northern Reaches have grown over the past four centuries, but outside of cities such as Threshold and Sizemordicus, it remains a frontier. The part of the Northern Reaches that you are currently in is called the Middenmark.

Below I show a snapshot of the Northern Reaches. The shaded area containing Rosen and Slaytonthorpe is a subregion known as the Principality of Brine. The shaded area containing Barrowmaze and Ironguard Motte is the Duchy of Aerik. Finally, the shaded area containing Kirkilston is the Middenmark, your current location.



Each hex in the screenshot above represents 5 miles from one face of a hex to the opposite face. If you were moving on foot, you could cover three hexes' distance in a full day of traveling. But escorting multiple caravan wagons, two hexes if you're lucky, the weather holds, and you aren't attacked.

I will be rolling for encounters during the journey. Encounters with wild creatures, monsters, or antagonistic humanoids can resolve into XP either through combat or through negotiation, outsmarting, or out-maneuvering. Gold recovered through travel, heists, or dungeoning can be spent to acquire XP toward leveling. Non-AI aided human-drawn mapping, world-building through roleplayed conversations, journaling in-character in a travel journal, building PC-to-PC and PC-to-NPC relationships: these sorts of things will garner XP.

You are on your way, exiting Kirkilston and heading northwest on the road connecting Kirkilston and Raven's Pass.

Ch 3 - Joining the Caravan to Raven's Pass

It quickly becomes apparent, even to the ones recently introduced to Urvan, that the young man is no seasoned traveller.

As they moved northward he seemed to keep up easily enough despite his chain hauberk, pack, shield and the mace at his belt, but there was something in his demeanor that spoke of straight lines and well swept halls as opposed to wilder places.

Indeed, he seemed especially fastidious when it came to his grooming and the state of his clothing, sometimes carefully peering over his white tabard with the red cross insignia for signs of imperfection during their roadside breaks.

It was not uncommon at times to see him attack a bit of road dust or pollen with a small brush a cloth, which were part of a kit he carried in a pouch on his belt.

It was difficult to say at a glance if this habit was mere vanity, the product of a particularly regimented lifestyle, or something else entirely but it seemed almost second nature to him.

“You know these lands well sir?” He asked Thurgan as he came to walk alongside him during the journey. “You were raised in the region perhaps?” he asked as cast a covert glance towards Narinza, as if not quite sure what to make of her.
