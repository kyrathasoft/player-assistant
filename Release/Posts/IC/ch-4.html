Ch 4 - Battle at Blightstone Pit

Sword raised, Kelpie stood firm as the orcspawn thundered toward him. The first brute crashed into his reach, and Kelpie met it with a downward swing every ounce of his strength behind the blow. Steel bit deep, the impact jarring up his arms.

There was no relief in him. No triumph. Only the grim knowledge that more were coming. He set his feet, jaw clenched tight. There were many more orcs hungry for slaughter. Kelpie lifted his sword again, shoulders squared, ready to meet them. If the caravan was to live, he would hold this line. If the day demanded blood, he would give his share. The hedge knight braced himself for the storm.
