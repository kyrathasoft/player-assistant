Ch 4 - Battle at Blightstone Pit

Jelb reached for a nearby rock and threw it at one of the orcs near the firey line, but not yet rounding the end of it.  He thought for a moment, then looked up at Thurgen with 4 orcs coming up toward him, 2 in roundabout fashion.  Human lives were at stake, he could not hold his dogs back any longer.

He pointed to the closest orcs going up toward Thurgen.  "Melbor, Kaybar.  Crash,"  He said less forcefully than he had previously, worried that he was now sending his dogs to their death.  But better them than Thurgen or one of the others here.

Ch 4 - Battle at Blightstone Pit

Seeing the danger on their flank, Kelpie broke from his position and rushed to replace Maximilian, sliding into the gap before the orcspawn could exploit it. He raised his shield, set his stance, and met the creature head on.

Steel flashed as he struck but the earlier strain in his arm betrayed him. His blade swept wide, cutting only air. Kelpie did not curse. He simply reset his guard, feet planted, shoulders squared. The hedge knight’s presence alone was enough to hold the line for a heartbeat longer.
