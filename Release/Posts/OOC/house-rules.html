Welcome & House Rules

Welcome to the Game!

If you acquire a hireling new to the party's history, you may assign your hireling's ability scores. These all start at 6 and you have 18 points you can spread to improve those -- with no hireling having any starting ability greater than 11. After creating the hireling character sheet, assign the hireling 1 hit point, then adjust upward if they enjoy a Constitution bonus.

Unlike PCs, hirelings only roll for possible ability score increases when they attain a new even-numbered level between 2 and 8, inclusive.
