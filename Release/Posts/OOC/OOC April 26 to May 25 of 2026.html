OOC

My first 3rd edition character was also my highest level ever.  Shem Whistler was a very diversified halfling rogue with a few low level magic spells.  We played almost every week for years (then occasionally after that) in 4-6 sessions, with annual 3-day weekend marathon games, rotating GMs by story arc.  He made it to the low 20's.

Shem was Chaotic Neutral, dubbed by the party Chaotic-shifty.  He provoked a Stealth/Sleight of Hand vs Perception arms race in the party, and provoked the party paladin to adopt the motto "Innocent until proven shifty."  He always rolled epically when the roll didn't matter, and fell 1-2 short on a d20 when it was important.

In the high teen levels he was investigating some slavers, found some and missed his sense motive by 1, then his perception by 1, then his initiative by 1, then his save vs poison by 1.  He woke up on a slave ship where the highest level enemy was ~lv 6.  They hadn't worried about him because he was obviously a completely inept low level commoner by their interactions.  He crushed them all, never missing or even being spotted until he let the last one see him and abandon ship.  Then he failed his roll to unify the other slaves, then failed his profession sailor check by 1, then botched his follow-up rolls.  Ended up crashing into an island of Mind Flayers... where he failed his save.  The party located his body later and had him reincarnated per his wishes.
