OOC May 25th Forward

As an observation and some feedback looking at the Mystic Theurge. I would think the XP requirement needs to be increased. A normal Magic-user learns one spell per level, the rules implies that the Mystic Theurge starts  at level 1 with knowing all the spells! That’s a massive advantage on top of gaining Cleric spells as level 2. The only downside is the change from 2500XP to 3000XP, that hardly a downside at all. Given the power level I would suggest more like 4000XP or even higher. As written the class is arguably stronger than an Elf, Elves pay heavily for their hybrid casting.

The example given doesn't line up either.

Mystic Theurge:
Example at Level 3:

Slots: 2 first level spells + 1 second level spell
Possible Preparation: Cure Light Wounds (Cleric) + Sleep (Wizard) + Bless (Cleric)


If the class follows Cleric spell progression they will not have access Bless at level three, because Clerics don't get 2nd level spells until level 4. If instead they gain Cleric spells at level 1, that’s another massive advantage.

Either way, the current Mystic Theurge:
Have more spell choice than a Magic-User
Casts more spells than a Cleric
Has access to both lists earlier
Pay almost no XP penalty for it

That effectively makes both core classes redundant.

Everyone’s definitely being creative, there are loads of interesting ideas flying around, which is great. But with so many options on the table, it’s hard to know where to begin.

My concerns are the same as before, and they boil down to what Gavin calls Class Niche Protection.
OSE:
In the classic Advanced game, there is a tendency for classes’ abilities to overlap significantly, leading to classes that have, for example, all of the abilities of a standard fighter or thief plus extra abilities of their own. Such classes are very powerful and tend to out-shine the classes whose abilities they share. The new classes in this book are designed with their own unique abilities and avoid encroaching on the niches of the standard classes described in Classic Fantasy:Genre Rules.


That’s the core issue: any new class needs to avoid overshadowing the four pillars of B/X play, Fighter, Cleric, Magic User, and Thief.

OSE caps all classes at 14th level. I don’t think we need to worry about anything beyond that. If we ever do need higher level progression BECMI exists as a reference, but that’s a problem for later.

So I suppose all I’m saying is as stated above, any new class needs to not overshadow the basics of Magic-user, Cleric, Thief and Fighter.

As an observation Magic-users are deliberately difficult to play especially as lower levels.

1 spell per day
1d4 hp
Only daggers
No armour
Slow XP progression
Spellbooks are vulnerable
They don’t get 6th level spells until very late

Anything that grants Magic-user spells without those restrictions needs to be balanced i.e. the elf, has two prime requisites, needs much more XP and doesn’t get level 6 spells.

The Mystic Theurge has the advantage of more weapon choice, a Magic-user can only use a dagger for 1d4 damage, anything doing more than 1d4 or has greater range is an advantage, they have both. Cleric and Magic-user spells both have their own niches and ways of working, having access to spells from both lists is a big thing.
