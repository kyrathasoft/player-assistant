World of Issenda - Scarlet Horizons

Old School Essentials, written by Necrotic Gnome, is the rules set. You can grab a copy of the basic rules as free resource over at the Necrotic Gnome website.

It's over a millenia since King Xavamros Battlebeard III linked up the Glittering Caves beneath Brokenspire Keep with Forge. Now, his grandson, Davian, is King of All Dwarves - yet Gundgathol still hasn't been reclaimed. It is rumored than a very large dwarven clan disappeared somewhere east of the Middenmark some three thousand years ago, and that a dark god was involved in this calamity.

In addition to any other XP bonuses due to exceptional ability scores, humans gain an additional +5% XP bonus over and above demi-human characters. There are no racial level limits, other than that all classes have a level cap of 36. Level 36 PCs do still improve skills, gain faction influence, advance in expertise, and exert political influence. Saving throws may improve, ultimately, to a value of 2.

The following breakdown shows where your XP will come from:

  • world-building (discovering new areas, mapping, recovering lost artifacts, contributing to world lore, etc. (20%)
  • victorious combat or outsmarting foes (10%)
  • founding and expanding settlements (20%)
  • completing minor story arcs (20%)
  • posting frequency (10%)
  • completing major story arcs (10%)
  • updating adventurer's journal / non-computerized (i.e., hand-written) mapping on the Miro board (10%)
