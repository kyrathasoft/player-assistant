[[Kelpie Lawfuller, Fighter|Kelpie]] is a bald, toothless man with the build of someone carved straight from the soil. Broad‘shouldered and sturdy, he carries the quiet strength of his lineage, the son of a humble hedge knight who taught him that honor is a mans true armor.  He is played by RPOL member Peace.
  
Born close to the land and raised among fields and furrows, Kelpie is a devoted follower of [[Demetra]], goddess of hearth and harvest. Her rites shape his worldview steady, dutiful, and rooted in simple virtues.  
  
Though his wits are plain and his learning modest, Kelpies sense of self is unshakeable. He knows who he is, what he stands for, and what he will not yield. In battle he is direct and dependable; in life he is grounded, loyal, and as enduring as the earth he so greatly reveres.
