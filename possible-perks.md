Fighter Perk Tree
Veteran Disciplines
Battlefield Awareness

Can never be surprised on a 1-in-6 surprise roll.

Veteran Reflexes

Requires Battlefield Awareness.

Once per combat, draw or ready a weapon instantly.

Command Voice

Friendly retainers gain +1 morale while the Fighter is visible.

Iron Reputation

Requires Command Voice.

Mercenaries and soldiers react as one step friendlier.

Weapon Masteries

Choose a weapon category.

Spear School

When using a spear against a charging foe, deal +1 damage.

Phalanx Discipline

Requires Spear School.

Gain +1 AC when adjacent to an ally with shield or spear.

Sword Precision

Once per combat reroll a single natural 1 on an attack.

Duelist's Eye

Requires Sword Precision.

Gain +1 AC against a single declared opponent.

Magic-User Perk Tree

These should improve versatility, not raw spell output.

Arcane Research
Mnemonic Glyphs

Can memorize one additional 1st-level spell in a spellbook after seeing it cast.

Not prepared. Merely recorded.

Rapid Notation

Requires Mnemonic Glyphs.

Copy spells into a spellbook at half normal time.

Living Index

Requires Rapid Notation.

Once per day locate a specific page in any book instantly.

Very useful for libraries and adventures.

Magical Efficiency
Careful Channeling

When rolling duration dice, reroll one die.

Economical Components

Ignore mundane spell components costing less than 1 gp.

Stable Formula

Requires Economical Components.

Once per day recast a spell that fizzled because of interruption.

Hidden Arcana
Candle Magic

Can perform harmless magical tricks around a flame.

Useful roleplaying utility.

Arcane Impression

Can determine whether a location was enchanted within the past day.

Residual Sight

Requires Arcane Impression.

May spend one turn studying an object to learn whether it has ever been magical.

Cleric Perk Tree
Sacred Authority
Consecrated Presence

Undead reaction rolls are one step worse.

Voice of Conviction

Requires Consecrated Presence.

Friendly NPCs sharing the faith gain +1 morale.

Saintly Reputation

Requires Voice of Conviction.

Shrines and temples provide free basic lodging.

Miracles of Service
Lay Caregiver

Recovering wounded allies gain an additional hit point after a night's rest if tended by the Cleric.

Herbal Benediction

Requires Lay Caregiver.

Create one blessed herbal poultice per day.

Restores 1 hp when used.

Pilgrim's Mercy

Requires Herbal Benediction.

Ignore one day's worth of natural environmental penalties during overland travel.

Spiritual Insight
Sense Desecration

Detect defiled holy ground after one turn of prayer.

Liturgical Scholar

Gain +2 on checks involving religious history.

Hagiographer

Requires Liturgical Scholar.

May identify saints, relics, and religious symbols automatically.

Thief Perk Tree

The Thief benefits most from perks because his abilities are naturally granular.

Urban Specialist
Fence Network

Can reliably locate a buyer for loot in towns.

Smuggler Contact

Requires Fence Network.

Can hide one item in any major settlement.

Shadow Syndicate

Requires Smuggler Contact.

May call in a modest favor from criminal contacts once per month.

Acrobatics
Catfall

Treat falls as 10 feet shorter.

Rolling Recovery

Requires Catfall.

Stand from prone without losing movement.

Rooftop Runner

Requires Rolling Recovery.

Move full speed across difficult urban terrain.

Infiltration
Silent Routine

Negate one failed Move Silently roll per day.

Familiar Layouts

Requires Silent Routine.

After ten minutes studying a building, ask the referee one useful security question.

Ghost Entry

Requires Familiar Layouts.

Once per adventure, automatically find an overlooked secondary entrance.

Ranger Perk Tree

These should emphasize wilderness expertise.

Pathfinder
Trail Sign

Can leave coded signs readable only by allies.

Fast Tracker

Requires Trail Sign.

Track at normal movement speed.

Master Pathfinder

Requires Fast Tracker.

Party becomes lost in wilderness only under exceptional conditions.

Hunter
Field Dressing

Harvest useful materials from beasts.

Preferred Quarry

Requires Field Dressing.

Select one creature type.

Gain +1 damage against it.

Legendary Hunter

Requires Preferred Quarry.

Track that creature type across impossible-looking terrain.

Survivalist
Weather Eye

Predict next day's weather.

Forager

Requires Weather Eye.

Party consumes half rations when foraging is possible.

Camp Master

Requires Forager.

Allies recover an additional hp during wilderness rest.

Cross-Class "Lost Secrets"

The most exciting perks are the ones anyone can discover.

Ancient Cartography

Travel speed +10% in mapped territory.

Dungeon Instinct

Once per dungeon level, ask whether a nearby room is inhabited.

Polyglot

Read one additional ancient language.

Beast Lore

Identify mundane creatures automatically.

Merchant's Acumen

Buy mundane goods at 10% discount.

Iron Constitution

Gain +1 bonus on saves versus disease and poison.

Legendary End-of-Tree Perks

These should feel special but never game-breaking.

Fighter: War Master's Presence

Retainers within sight never check morale due to casualties.

Magic-User: Invisible Equations

Invent one unique cantrip.

Cleric: Minor Relic Keeper

Consecrate a single holy object that performs one tiny miracle daily.

Thief: Name Without Shadow

Ordinary witnesses forget details of the Thief after one day.

Ranger: Friend of the Wild

One natural animal encountered per day begins neutral rather than hostile.

These perks are valuable because they expand a character's sphere of competence rather than increase raw numbers. In my experience, that keeps OSE's class balance intact while still giving players the thrill of discovering secret techniques, lost traditions, and hidden knowledge.
