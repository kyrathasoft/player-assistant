## Ch 1 - Kirkilston

- The party organizes roles before leaving Kirkilston:
  - Mapper: Slip, tentative.
  - Caller: Kelpie, tentative.
  - Quartermaster: Urvan.
  - Chronicler: Jelb, tentative and later confirmed.
- The immediate objective is to seek Nuanda the Good Witch, who lives less than an hour from Kirkilston.
- Kelpie takes the fore as the group leaves the village by the southeastern path.
- A wild boar charges the party in the forest.
- Kelpie holds the front line while Jelb commands Melbor and Kaybar to attack.
- The dogs badly wound the boar, and Urvan lands the killing blow.
- The party briefly butchers the enormous boar, prioritizing useful cuts and gifts for Nuanda rather than a full field dressing.
- The group reaches Nuanda's moss-covered cottage in the fog-laden forest.
- Nuanda welcomes the party, asks them to help prepare stew, and recognizes their urgency.
- Kelpie explains that Jelenneth, daughter of Farmer Heldrith and Parker Laneworth, is missing from north of Kirkilston.
- Nuanda asks whether the party has an item belonging to Jelenneth, since that would make her search easier.
- The chapter closes by sending play to Chapter 2.

## Ch 2 - Supper With Nuanda

- Jelb produces Jelenneth's glove, given to him by her brother for tracking purposes.
- Nuanda is pleased because the glove lets her seek Jelenneth more effectively through the Dream World.
- The party stays overnight at Nuanda's cottage.
- During supper:
  - Nuanda discusses white witches, black witches, and the destructive powers used by the latter.
  - Urvan asks careful questions from the perspective of the Red Bannermen.
  - Jelb speaks about law, good, and the kind of god he seeks.
  - Kelpie shares elements of his difficult past and his devotion to Demetra.
- Nuanda prepares wards and instructs the party in words meant to call her back from the Shadow or Dream World if needed.
- Morrow, Nuanda's black cat, is sent outside to guard against elves and other threats.
- Jelb carefully observes the magic and repeats Nuanda's return phrase.
- The night passes safely.
- In the morning, Nuanda reports what she learned:
  - Jelenneth was abducted by bandits while picking berries near her family's property.
  - She was taken northward.
  - Nuanda contacted Kuiper, a ranger ally who ranges between Raven's Pass, Threshold, Slaytonthorpe, the Coal Hills, Coalscuttle, and nearby settlements.
  - Multiple communities have suffered abductions and disappearances.
- Nuanda identifies the Blanryde Hills as a likely destination or lead, west of the Coal Hills in the Slaytonthorpe area under Count Parlfray.
- Nuanda gives the party a map and asks them to obtain ritual components:
  - An amethyst.
  - A sprig of Hwyanthemon.
  - Hair from a slain orc.
- Nuanda recommends joining a caravan from Kirkilston to Raven's Pass instead of traveling alone.
- She gives the party hard biscuits and honey for the road.
- She advises them to ask Nimba at The Mason's Apron in Raven's Pass for an introduction to Samuel the blacksmith.
- The party agrees to take caravan guard work and continue the search.
- The chapter closes by sending play to Chapter 3.

## Ch 3 - Joining the Caravan to Raven's Pass

- The party is traveling with Billworth Turgen's caravan toward Raven's Pass.
- The available chapter file opens with the party responding to signs of orcspawn activity and a horn call.
- Kelpie argues for moving toward Blightstone Pit rather than racing to Raven's Pass with incomplete information.
- Thurgan sends woodsmen to locate Urvan, Maximilian, and Narinza and guide them toward the quarry rendezvous.
- Melbor and Kaybar alert Jelb and help locate the separated companions.
- The party reunites and pushes the caravan hard toward Blightstone Pit.
- Billworth drives the mules faster while Thurgan coordinates the rear guard.
- Near the abandoned quarry, at least two dozen orcs emerge from the forest behind the caravan.
- The caravan attempts to outrun the ambush.
- Jelb casts an illusion of a rockslide at a choke point to disrupt the pursuit.
- Thurgan and caravan guards fire into the attackers while the wagons rush forward.
- Some caravan guards are killed by orc arrows.
- Urvan and Narinza are cut off by orcs as the wagons swing wide of the pit.
- Kelpie, Geoffroy, Maximilian, Jelb, and the dogs prepare to hold a defensive position near the rock wall.
- Jelb distributes flasks of oil to guards and prepares fire as part of the defensive plan.
- The chapter closes by sending play to Chapter 4.

## Ch 4 - Battle at Blightstone Pit

- The battle continues at Blightstone Pit.
- Jelb throws a rock, then sends Melbor and Kaybar against orcs threatening Thurgan.
- The dogs flank and kill an orc.
- Kelpie kills another orc.
- Geoffroy and Fluffy fight aggressively at the line.
- Maximilian uses his sling, eventually killing an orc with a headshot.
- Jelb casts Color Spray against two orcs near the western end of the wagons, disabling them for several rounds.
- Kelpie swaps forward to protect Maximilian, who is poorly suited for melee.
- The party works to clear the northern path while holding off the remaining orcs.
- A huge tentacled creature begins climbing out of Blightstone Pit.
- The orc leader orders a retreat, saying they can find others to offer to "The Deep Friends."
- The creature snatches remaining orcs from the quarry floor.
- The party shifts from fighting to escaping before the creature fully emerges.
- The caravan begins turning away from the pit.
- Kelpie helps drag and force the wagons clear while others scramble aboard or help the flight.
- The chapter closes by sending play to Chapter 5.

## Ch 5 - A Betentacled Escape

- The party has escaped the immediate danger at Blightstone Pit and studies a scroll Jelb recovered from a slain orc.
- Thurgan identifies the Toothbreakers as a large orcish clan from the hills above Milborne.
- The scroll references "Deep Friends" and a "Red Tusk," suggesting an organized power behind the orc activity.
- The group discusses whether Urvan was killed, taken by orcs, or otherwise separated, and whether Narinza might be involved.
- Thurgan mentions old suspicions that the drow were involved in the departure of the surface elves from places like the Glittering Canopy and Aelith-Calaer.
- Algorn Druff arrives from the south, sent by Nuanda's magic after she scried the party's progress.
- Algorn examines the scroll and concludes it had two authors:
  - One knowledgeable author who listed desired captives such as a Red Bannerman, a Drow of Night Below, and a half elf or elf of the ancient line.
  - One less sophisticated orcish author handling more direct orders or additions.
- Kelpie argues that the orcspawn may be connected to Jenny's disappearance and that following Jenny's trail may also reveal what happened to Urvan and Narinza.
- The group chooses to continue toward Raven's Pass rather than return to the pit.
- Geoffroy speculates about treasure in the tentacled creature's lair.
- Algorn notes that making Blightstone Pit safe again could be enormously valuable because the quarry still contains years of usable stone.
- Thurgan leads the surviving caravan into an overnight camp with the wagons circled in a defensible clearing.
- Around camp, Algorn discusses possible surface factions using orcs as muscle or fodder, including:
  - The Basilisk's Claw.
  - The Cult of the Dragon.
  - The Zhentarim.
- Jelb searches his memory for what he knows about those factions and studies the scroll for clues about authorship.
- The caravan passes safely through the night.
- By late morning, the group reaches outlying farmsteads near Raven's Pass.
- Local farmers report that town authorities have sent runners south to Milborne and Threshold for aid against orc problems.
- The caravan reaches Raven's Pass and delivers the remaining goods.
- Billworth Turgen treats the party to a meal at The Mason's Apron and pays each adventurer fifteen gold coins in thanks.
- Kelpie prepares for the next leg into the Blanryde Hills.
- Maximilian worries about Urvan, Narinza, and the growing scale of the opposition.

## Active Leads and Open Threads

- Jelenneth "Jenny" remains missing after being abducted by bandits and carried north.
- The Blanryde Hills are the next major destination for the search.
- Nuanda's requested components remain outstanding:
  - Amethyst.
  - Hwyanthemon.
  - Hair from a slain orc.
- The recovered orc scroll links the Toothbreakers, the Deep Friends, Red Tusk, and a second non-orc author.
- The enemy appears interested in specific valuable captives, including a Red Bannerman, a Drow of Night Below, and someone of ancient elven blood.
- Urvan and Narinza's fate remains uncertain.
- The tentacled creature beneath Blightstone Pit is a major unresolved threat and possible treasure site.
- Surface factions may be manipulating the orcs, with Algorn naming the Basilisk's Claw, the Cult of the Dragon, and the Zhentarim as possibilities.
- Raven's Pass, The Mason's Apron, Nimba, and Samuel the blacksmith are immediate support points.
