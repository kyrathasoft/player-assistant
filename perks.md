Possibility for basic mechanics:

-Perk trees cost 3 Perk Points (PP from now on) per level as a base but must be acquired sequentially, individual perks cost 5 PP as a base number that may be raised or lowered based on actual power.  A given character can only have one started but incomplete perk tree, though they may obtain individual perks while still working on a tree.

-Perks can be taken when granted or bought with PP.  Individual perks (though not those in a tree) can be declined when obtained.  If an perk is declined, the player receives half (round up) of the value of the perk.

-Perk points are accrued weekly (1 per week) and additional perk points are gained when a character engages in thematically appropriate activities in character or in OOC contributions.


For some starter perk ideas: mostly thinking story related, character related, or quality of life stuff.

Gripper of the Grody (Individual Perk): Geoffrey has touched (and eaten!) things that would kill a lesser man.  He has obtained a permanent +1 on saves against Death/Disease/Poison.

Spell Secrets of [Deity] (Devotion Tree): [Cleric] has learned another of the ancient secrets and rites of [deity].  One thematic spell of a level equal to the level of this Devotion is added from another spellcasting list to the cleric spell list for this character only.
->So at first devotion level a level 1 spell, at second level a level 2 spell is added as well, etc.

The Three Stances of General Vask (Technique Tree): [Fighter] has discovered the [first/second/third] stance of the Mad General Vask, whose strategies were always made up on the spot, and were almost always more effective than would have been expected.
Level One: Charge of the Mad - [Fighter] has gained a new option for combat movement: they may leave melee range of an opponent and attack in the same turn, but in doing so allow the opponent to make one free attack on them.
Level Two: Ranged attack in melee with a penalty?
Level Three: Throwing melee weapon?

[Ancient/Famous Mage or Cleric Name]'s Mastery of Minor Magic (Individual Arcana or Devotion): [Character] has discovered a minor and effortless trick of magic.  They learn a 1st level spell from their list and once per day may cast that specific without preparing it or using a spell slot.  To qualify as a minor and effortless, the spell must have no direct combat applications.  So Detect Magic, Detect Good/Evil, Light, and Purify Food & Drink could be chosen, but Cure Light Wounds, Protection from Evil, and Remove Fear could not.

Nildor's Nudge (Individual Knack): [Thief] has picked up one of the tricks of Nildor, a proverbial halfling rogue who is storied for succeeding at the improbable, if only just. For consideration (feel free to thumbs-up or thumbs-down any of these):

Here are 24 new Perks, split evenly across five classes (5 for Fighter, 5 for Magic-User, 5 for Cleric, 5 for Thief, and 4 for Ranger) plus 1 additional Cross-Class Lost Secret (to round out the 24).

These focus on expanding options, utility, story hooks, and tactical flexibility rather than inflating flat power stats.

Fighter Perks
Technique Tree: The Three Stances of General Vask
[Continuation & Completion of your starter concept]

Stance of the Mad General: Level 2 — Point-Blank Volley

Requires Charge of the Mad.

Effect: The Fighter may make a ranged attack against an opponent while engaged in melee without drawing a free attack, provided they accept a -2 penalty to their attack roll.

Stance of the Mad General: Level 3 — Unorthodox Pitch

Requires Point-Blank Volley.

Effect: The Fighter may hurl any non-heavy melee weapon (e.g., longsword, battleaxe, morningstar) at a target up to 20 feet away as a ranged attack. On a hit, the weapon deals its standard damage plus 1 extra point, but is considered dropped at the target's feet.

Individual Discipline Perks
Shield Wall Veteran

Effect: When wielding a shield, the Fighter may spend a round holding position to grant an adjacent ally an additional +1 AC against missile fire until the Fighter’s next turn.

Slayer’s Eye

Effect: When examining a slain monster or foe, the Fighter can instantly identify its primary attack method, approximate hit dice, and whether it possessed any supernatural resistances.

Magic-User Perks
Tree: The Codex of Transmutative Alchemy
Lead to Tin

Effect: The Magic-User can alter the weight or density of a single non-magical object under 10 lbs for 1 hour, making it either twice as heavy or half as light. Useful for encumbrance or triggering pressure plates.

Reagent Distillation

Requires Lead to Tin.

Effect: By expending 10 gp of mundane material components during a rest, the Magic-User can brew a flask of volatile acid, alchemist's fire, or anti-toxin without specialized lab equipment.

Transmutative Resonance

Requires Reagent Distillation.

Effect: Once per day, when the Magic-User casts a spell that affects an object (e.g., Knock, Hold Portal, Light), they may double the spell's duration or range.

Individual Arcana Perks
Scribe’s Intuition

Effect: The Magic-User can read non-magical ciphers, hidden codes, and damaged/smudged mundane texts at normal reading speed without making a check.

Resonant Focus

Effect: The Magic-User chooses a small, non-magical personal trinket (e.g., an carved bone die, a silver ring). While holding this item, they receive +1 on saving throws against direct magical damage.

Cleric Perks
Devotion Tree: Faith of the Shieldbearer
Sanctified Ward

Effect: Once per day, the Cleric may trace a holy glyph on a doorway, chest, or threshold. Fiends and Undead must pass a Save vs. Spell to cross or touch the warded object for the next 8 hours.

Vigilant Shepherd

Requires Sanctified Ward.

Effect: Whenever an ally within 15 feet fails a saving throw against fear, charm, or mind-affecting magic, the Cleric may expend a turn attempt to allow that ally to immediately reroll the save.

Aura of the Unbroken

Requires Vigilant Shepherd.

Effect: Retainers and hirelings under the Cleric's direct command gain a +2 bonus to Morale checks when defending a holy site, shrine, or consecrated area.

Individual Devotion Perks
Anointing of the Dead

Effect: Performing a 1-turn funeral rite over a corpse prevents it from being raised as an undead creature by any spell short of high-level divine magic or dark rituals.

Censer Bearer

Effect: The Cleric can burn holy incense during a rest. Anyone resting within the scented area gains a +2 bonus on saving throws against disease and poison for the next 24 hours.

Thief Perks
Tree: Shadows of the Underworld
Whispered Rumor

Effect: After spending one hour in a tavern or market, the Thief can automatically learn the prevailing local rumors regarding one specific target, noble house, or faction.

Underbelly Jargon

Requires Whispered Rumor.

Effect: The Thief can communicate simple secret messages to other rogue types or shady NPCs using subtle hand gestures and criminal slang without surrounding observers noticing.

Slip the Noose

Requires Underbelly Jargon.

Effect: Once per adventure, if the Thief is captured, bound, or imprisoned, they automatically discover a hidden flaw in their restraints or cell (e.g., a loose brick, a rusted lock, a hidden hairclip) within 1 turn.

Individual Knack Perks
Light Fingers, Fast Sleeves

Effect: The Thief can conceal a small, palm-sized object (a key, a potion vial, a gem) on their person so thoroughly that standard physical searches will fail to find it unless stripped entirely naked.

Deadfall Instinct

Effect: When a mechanical trap is triggered by any member of the party within 10 feet of the Thief, the Thief receives a +2 bonus on their saving throw to avoid its effects.

Ranger Perks
Fieldcraft Tree: Frontier Tactics
Spur the Pack

Effect: Mounts, beasts of burden, and hunting dogs under the Ranger’s direct care travel 20% faster on overland journeys without incurring fatigue.

Guerilla Sight

Requires Spur the Pack.

Effect: When setting up a wilderness ambush, the party gains a +1 bonus to surprise enemy groups if the Ranger spent at least 1 turn scouting the terrain beforehand.

Individual Fieldcraft Perks
Wilderness Remedy

Effect: Once per day in the wild, the Ranger can forage for specific medicinal herbs to brew a tea that cures mundane non-magical sickness or reduces fever overnight for one person.

Beast Mastery: Signals

Effect: The Ranger can train a single non-monstrous animal (such as a hawk, hound, or horse) to perform up to three complex tactical signals or commands (e.g., scout ahead, raise an alarm, retrieve an item).

Cross-Class "Lost Secrets" (Bonus)
Deep Vault Cartography

Effect: When mapping a underground dungeon, the character can spot structural patterns and has a 2-in-6 chance to intuit the direction toward the nearest staircase leading up or down.
