A phenomenon in which seawater coming shoreward on the tide is tinted scarlet, from the shore and seemingly out to sea even to the far horizon. [[Welcome to the Scarlet Horizons campaign|Scarlet Horizons]] are much feared because they herald a period of madness among intelligent creatures that come within half a mile of this phenomenon, and aberrant behavior even across creatures of only animal intellect. It is rumored this phenomenon was first recorded in [[Post-Ravaging|PR]] 196 and that it has even been found in some very large inland [[Lakes|lakes]] since PR 201. Curiously, [[The Shou|Shou]] seem highly resistant to the effects of this Red Tide. This tide always carries [[Magic|magical]] emanations, yet also seems to be a naturally-occurring phenomenon.
