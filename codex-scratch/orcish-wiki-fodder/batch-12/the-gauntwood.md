An area of forest in the vicinity of [[Slaytonthorpe]]. Beneath this forest lies the ancient dwarven settlement known as the [[The Gauntwood]] Galleries. The Gauntwood is the southerneastern-most portion of the Thornwood. This woods bounds Slaytonthorpe to the northeast, east, and south.
