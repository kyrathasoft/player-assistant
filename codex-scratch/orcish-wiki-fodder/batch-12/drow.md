Dark elves who once, many millennia ago, were part of the great throng of surface elves. Sometime in the distant past, a great dispute and schism divided the elves of the surface world. The elves will not speak of it outside of their own race. Those who withdrew to the [Underdark](https://bryanmiller.us/myrpg/html/terminology.html#underdark) eventually became _dark elves_, able to use [[Faerzress|faerzress]]-induced native abilities.
