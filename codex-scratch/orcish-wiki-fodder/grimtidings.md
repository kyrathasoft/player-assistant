Annual observance of the [[Duergar]] on December 21 in which they lay down their tools and gather to hear stories of their voluntary exile and the decadence of other dwarves. [[Laduguer]] is praised for his great skill, and the [[Duergar]] swear vengeance against those who have insulted their [[Gods|god]] and their people.
