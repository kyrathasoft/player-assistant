Owner of [[Turgen's Trade Goods]] and local representative of the Silver Standard Caravan Company in [[Helix]]. Billworth facilitates shipments to [[Ironguard Motte]] and imports from the same. At 55 years old, Turgen is quite well-off financially. He's an unclassed expert merchant with 4 HP. He has a mustache, speaks in a bass voice, smokes a pipe, and is a likable fellow who just became a grandfather in the Spring of 203 [[Post-Ravaging|PR]].  

Merchant 4  
Hp 4  
Str 7  
Dex 7  
Con 10  
Int 9  
Wis 6  
Cha 11

When traveling, Billworth is accompanied by his nephew Thurgan Newl, a 2nd level ranger, and his grandson, Berric, a 3rd level fighter. If traveling with one of his  caravans, he will also have 1d4+4 guards that may be mercenaries or adventurers, or a mix of the two. He sources NPC guards from the [[Silver Standard Trading Company]] because they're well-trained. Sometimes, however, he will hire adventurers because they'll usually work for less pay.
