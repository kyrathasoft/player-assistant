The proprietor of [[Reteps Relics]] and secretly guildmaster of the **Darkeyes**, the thieves guild of [[Eastdale]]/[[Middenmark]]. He deals in antiquities and precious-metal objects, but his shop fronts for the underground network that profits from the [[Red Laws]] by moving alcohol, gambling, prostitution, and discreet passage through the sewers.

[[Mordicar Justhand]] is no fool, nor is his spy network ineffectual. He knows of this black market and directs considerable resources toward finding its mastermind.
