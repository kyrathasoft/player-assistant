[[Bruce Bancroft|Bruce]] "Beer-Brewer" Bancroft is the human proprietor on the [[Stonehill Inn]] in [[Phandalin]]. He sneers at the [[Red Laws|Red Laws']] prohibition on drinking alcohol. Bruce has developed a friendship with the leader of the [[Inglorious Bastards]], [[Maven Dursk]], a [[Human|human]] female thief. The fact that she is extremely comely may be a significant influence on this development.  For Bruce, [[Total Abstinence|abstinence]] is a condition not to be tolerated.
