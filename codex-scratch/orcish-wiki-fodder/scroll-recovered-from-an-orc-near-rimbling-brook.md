**TOOTH-BREAKER ORDERS**  
**For the Taking of Two-Legs**

By command of Red-Tusk and the Deep Friends.

Strong ones are needed. The Deep Friends pay well. Weaklings are not worth rope or food.

---

### Places to Watch

**Stone Road East of Kirkliston**

- Merchant wagons pass every third or fourth day.
- Look for robe-wearers, map-carriers, and old men with books.
- One book-man escaped before. Break legs if needed.

**The Raven's Pass Trail**

- Travelers often move in small groups.
- Take the thinker before the fighters if possible.
- If no thinker is present, take the largest fighter.

**The Old Shrine by the Stream**

- Wandering priests and hedge-wizards stop there.
- Easy to seize at night.
- Leave no tracks.

**South Farmsteads**

- Good source of labor stock.
- Young and healthy preferred.
- Do not waste rope on the old.

---

### Desired Captives

**First Choice**

- Wizards
- Sages
- Priests
- Readers of strange books
- Those carrying scrolls

**Second Choice**

- Smiths
- Carters
- Miners
- Strong fighters
- Men and women with broad shoulders

**Do Not Take**

- Sickly
- Crippled
- Very young
- Very old

---

### Questions for Captives

Ask until answers are given:

- Where are the old tunnels?
- What lies beneath the broken stone?
- Who keeps maps of the deep places?
- Where are the sealed doors?
- Who knows the language of the dark ones?
- Who has seen the black pit?

If they lie:

- Remove food.
- Use iron.
- Ask again.

---

### Delivery Instructions

Travel only by night.

Avoid the road north.

Cross the dead stream and follow the broken stones.

Leave offerings at the watcher-mark.

Bring all captives to the **Cold Mouth**.

The Cold Mouth lies:

> Beyond the last fields.  
> Beyond the thorn hills.  
> North of the village of men.  
> Where the earth split and never healed.  
> Where the black water runs beneath the roots.

The Deep Friends will collect them there.
