Used in the context of a tabletop roleplaying game such as [Scarlet Horizons]([http://bryanmiller.us/scarlet-horizons](https://rpol.net/game.php?gi=80170&date=1779172065)), the word denotes a series of successive adventures with some unifying theme or trigger. Conversely, a series of unrelated adventures is comprised of _one-offs_.
