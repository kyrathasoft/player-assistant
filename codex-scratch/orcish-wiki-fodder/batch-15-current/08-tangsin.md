A mild, smoked drug popular throughout the [[Northern Reaches|Reaches]], _tangsin_ is a mixture of tobacco, ginseng, and the leaf of a hyoscyamine-containing plant. Tangsin tends to relieve intestinal and allergy symptoms in [[Humanoids|humans]], elves, and half-elves. These effects are less notable in [[Gnome|gnomes]], halflings, an dwarves ” but it is significantly beneficial in treating hypertension in [[Gnome|gnomes]] and halflings. No broadly effective treatment of hypertension has been discovered for dwarves. The majority of [[Human|human]], elven, and half-elven users choose to use tangsin for the pleasant taste and mild feelings of euphoric connection it engenders.
