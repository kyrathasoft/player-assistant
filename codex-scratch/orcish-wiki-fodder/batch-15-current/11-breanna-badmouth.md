A 3'4" female Halfling farmer of the first century [[Post-Ravaging|PR]] who sold her 10-acre farm on the western outskirts of [[Phandalin]] to the PCs and remained as overseer. She helped take care of [[Tipsy]] and [[Topsy]] when the PCs were out adventuring. Her last name is properly pronounced 'bah-mooth', but the locals ” mainly to tease [[Breanna Badmouth|Breanna]] ” pronounced it in a way that suggested the halfling used profanity and off-color humor. She _didn't_, at least not very often.
