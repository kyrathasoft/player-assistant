**Set**  
God of Serpents, Death, and the Underworld  
Alignment: Chaotic  
Symbol: Two snake heads facing each other

Set and his brother [Orcus](https://bryanmiller.us/myrpg/forces/powers/orcus.html) deposed their father [Nergal](https://bryanmiller.us/myrpg/forces/powers/nergal.html) for control of the underworld. After completing the task, they turned on each other to establish complete control over their father's spheres of power. A war for control of the underworld began and has spilled over into the mortal realm. Followers of Set are committed to the destruction of Orcus and his faithful.
