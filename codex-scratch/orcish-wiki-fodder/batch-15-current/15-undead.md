The undead are beings in mythology, legend, or fiction that are deceased but behave as if alive. Most commonly the term refers to corporeal forms of formerly alive [[Human]], such as [[Mummy|mummies]], [[Vampire|vampires]], and [[Zombie|zombies]], which have been reanimated by supernatural means, [[technology]], or disease. In some cases (for example, in Dungeons & [[Dragonkind|Dragons]], the term also includes incorporeal forms of the dead, such as ghosts).
